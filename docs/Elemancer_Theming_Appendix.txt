# Appendix A: Thematic Considerations for the Elemancer Class

## 1. Core Concept: The "Manifestation" of Will
The Elemancer is distinct from the academic Mage or the faithful Cleric. Their power is neither learned from a book nor granted by a deity; it is **imposed** upon reality through sheer force of personality and physical endurance.

The term **"Manifest"** was chosen to replace "Sing" or "Cast" because it captures the act of **instantiating material out of nothing**. It implies that the fire or earth was not summoned from elsewhere, but was forced into existence by the Elemancer's determination.

### Thematic Keywords:
*   **Instantiate:** The element appears because the Elemancer wills it to be so.
*   **Ad Lib:** The performance is not a rigid ritual. One Elemancer might scream the fire into existence; another might dance it into a frenzy; another might simply glare until the air combusts. The mechanic honors this by being a sustained, unique expression.
*   **Drive:** The resource cost (Movement Points) represents the physical toll of this "Drive". It is exhausting to hold reality in a shape it does not want to be.

## 2. Terminology Shift
To reinforce this theme, the following terminology changes are adopted:

| Old Term (Bard/Mage) | New Term (Elemancer) | Thematic Implication |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Sing / Cast** | **Manifest** | Active creation. "I make this real." |
| **Song / Spell** | **Manifestation** | The result of the will. A sustained reality. |
| **Mana** | **Will / Endurance** | (Mechanically MP) The fuel is internal stamina, not external magic. |
| **Concentration** | **Focus** | The act of maintaining the Manifestation against disruption. |

## 3. Visual Language & Player Experience
The output text for the class should reflect the "unique expression" of the player. Instead of generic "You cast...", the system should evoke the physical nature of the act.

**Examples:**
*   *“You grit your teeth and **manifest** the crushing density of the earth.”*
*   *“Sweat runs down your back as you **drive** the wind into a frenzy.”*
*   *“With a sharp gesture, you **impose** stillness upon the room, manifesting a vacuum.”*

## 4. Architectural Fit
This theming aligns with the mechanical decision to use **Movement Points**.
*   **Why MP?** Because "Will" is exhausting. Just as a sprinter tires from running, an Elemancer tires from *forcing* the world to change.
*   **Why Pulse-based?** Because the Manifestation is not a fire-and-forget formula. It is a weight being held up by the Elemancer's mind. If they stop pushing (stop paying the cost), the Manifestation collapses.

## 5. Conclusion
The Elemancer is the "Virtuoso of Reality." They do not ask the elements for help; they **are** the elements, expressed through the lens of their unique spirit.
