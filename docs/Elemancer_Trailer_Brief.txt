# Director's Brief: "The Elemancer" Game Trailer
**Project:** RavenMUD Class Update Trailer
**Total Runtime:** ~40 Seconds
**Style:** Cinematic, High-Contrast, Epic Fantasy
**Audio Vibe:** Dynamic range. Heavy lows (Earth/Fire) cutting to silence/highs (Ice/Air).

---

## Asset Checklist
*   **Video Files:** 5 Clips (Dwarf, Fire Elemental, Elf, Gnome, Human).
*   **Audio:** Voiceover (VO) track.
*   **Text:** Title Card overlay.

---

## Editing Timeline

### **0:00 - 0:06 | Scene 1: The Anchor (Earth)**
*   **Visual:** Dwarf transforming into stone armor.
*   **Edit Note:** Fade in from black (0.5s). Focus on the texture of the stone spreading.
*   **Color Grade:** Desaturated, cold, except for the glowing purple crystals.
*   **Audio:** Deep, rumbling bass drone. The sound of heavy rocks grinding together (crunchy/heavy).
*   **Voiceover:** "In a world of studied magic... and borrowed prayers..."

### **0:06 - 0:12 | Scene 2: The Living Forge (Fire)**
*   **Visual:** Fire Elemental empowering the party in the dungeon.
*   **Transition:** **Hard Cut** on a drum hit or impact sound.
*   **Edit Note:** Sync the "ignition" of the sword/arrows with a visual flare or flash frame.
*   **Color Grade:** High contrast. Deep blues for the dungeon, blown-out oranges/whites for the fire.
*   **Audio:** Sudden, loud roar of a blast furnace or bonfire. Sizzling sound when magic hits weapons.
*   **Voiceover:** "...true power is not asked for. It is... unleashed."

### **0:12 - 0:18 | Scene 3: The Breath (Ice)**
*   **Visual:** Elf exhaling frost on the frozen lake.
*   **Transition:** **Hard Cut** to silence. The "audio vacuum" effect.
*   **Edit Note:** Slow motion feel. Focus on the particles of ice hanging in the air.
*   **Color Grade:** Cool blues, cyans, and white. Ethereal glow.
*   **Audio:** Cut the fire sound instantly. Replace with a high-pitched, hollow wind whistle and delicate tinkling of glass/ice.
*   **Voiceover:** "The elements do not merely serve you. They *are* you."

### **0:18 - 0:24 | Scene 4: The Whisper (Air/Stealth)**
*   **Visual:** Gnome blowing the wind whisper to distract the guard.
*   **Transition:** Cross-dissolve (fast) or wipe.
*   **Edit Note:** Emphasize the guard turning his head. Maybe a subtle zoom-in on the Gnome's smirk.
*   **Color Grade:** Night-time stealth. Dark grays and shadows.
*   **Audio:** Silence mostly. Subtle crickets. A soft "whoosh" of air. The heavy metal "clank" of the guard turning.
*   **Voiceover:** "A whisper on the wind... can topple empires."

### **0:24 - 0:32 | Scene 5: The Transformation (Terraform)**
*   **Visual:** Human overwriting the gray city with green jungle.
*   **Transition:** Match cut or fast zoom out.
*   **Edit Note:** This is the climax. Speed ramp if necessary to make the jungle growth look explosive.
*   **Color Grade:** Start gray/drab -> Bloom into vibrant saturation as the green spreads.
*   **Audio:** Orchestral swell (Strings/Horns). The sound of trees growing rapidly (wood snapping, leaves rustling). Thunder rumble.
*   **Voiceover:** "Reshape the earth. Ignite the soul. Become the conduit."

### **0:32 - 0:40 | Scene 6: Title Card**
*   **Visual:** Black background. 
*   **Text 1 (Center):** **RAVENMUD** (Stone texture).
*   **Text 2 (Below):** **THE ELEMANCER** (Glowing Elemental font).
*   **Text 3 (Small):** "Play Today."
*   **Audio:** Music fades out. A final deep, resonating "Thrum" sound (like a movie trailer hit).
*   **Voiceover:** "The Elemancer. Play RavenMUD today."
