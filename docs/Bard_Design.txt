# Bard Class Design Document

## Overview
The Bard (ID 11) is a utility and support class that leverages Charisma (CHA) and music to manipulate the environment and empower allies.

## Core Mechanics

### 1. Song System (ID 351-359)
*   **Type:** Aura-based pulses.
*   **Command:** `sing <song name>`
*   **Cost:** Drains Movement Points (MP) per pulse (uses `mana_min` as pulse cost).
*   **Scaling:** Effects scale with level.
*   **Restriction:** Only one song can be active at a time.
*   **Interference:** Any command stops the current song. Movement stops songs flagged with `SST_MOVEMENT`.

#### Implemented Songs:
*   **Minor Refreshment (351):** Level 1. Small HP regen pulse.
*   **Bravery (352):** Level 5. Group Hitroll/Damroll buff.
*   **Healing (353):** Level 12. Group HP regen pulse.
*   **Protection (354):** Level 18. Group AC bonus.
*   **Silence (355):** Level 25. Chance to silence enemies in room.
*   **Discord (356):** Level 32. Damage pulse to enemies.
*   **Speed (357):** Level 40. Group Haste buff.
*   **Revelations (358):** Level 45. Group Detect Invisibility.
*   **Hero (359):** Level 50. Massive group buff (Hit/Dam/MaxHP).

### 2. Wayfarer's Step (ID 296)
*   **Type:** Passive.
*   **Level:** 1.
*   **Effect:** Reduces movement point cost of traversing rooms by 50% (minimum 1).
*   **Implementation:** Hooked into `do_simple_move` in `movement.c`.

### 3. Ventriloquate (Level 10)
*   **Type:** Tactical Skill (ID 41).
*   **Command:** `ventriloquate <room direction> <sound direction>`
*   **Effect:** Projects sound into an adjacent room to lure aggressive mobs.
*   **Mechanic:** Aggressive, non-sentinel mobs must save vs Spell or move in the specified sound direction.
*   **Cost:** 20 Mana.

### 4. Scouting & Utility
*   **Scan:** Available at level 10 (Skill ID 194).
*   **Standard Spells:** Bards have access to Invisible, Haste, and Silence at appropriate levels.
