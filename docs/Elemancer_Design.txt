# Elemancer Design Document
# Replaces Class: Bard (ID 11)

## Overview
The **Elemancer** is a conduit of primal forces. Unlike wizards who study arcane formulas or clerics who pray to deities, the Elemancer draws fire, ice, earth, and wind directly from their own **Force of Will** (Charisma). They channel these volatile energies through rhythmic chants, dances, and intense focus.

This class replaces the "Bard" prototype. It retains the unique **Movement Point (MV)** resource mechanic (representing physical and mental exhaustion from channeling) but shifts the theme from "Music/Support" to "Elemental Channeling/Environment".

## Core Stats
*   **Primary Attribute:** **Charisma (CHA)**. Represents the force of personality required to bind elemental forces to one's will.
*   **Secondary Attribute:** **Constitution (CON)**. Required to sustain the physical toll of channeling (High Max Move).
*   **Resource:** **Willpower** (represented mechanically by **Movement Points**).

## Core Mechanic: Elemental Invocations (The "Song" System)
Elemancers do not "cast" in the traditional sense. They **Invoke** rites.
*   **Command:** `invoke <rite name>` (Replaces `sing`)
*   **Mechanic:**
    *   Initiating a Rite costs an initial amount of Will (Move).
    *   Maintaining a Rite drains Will (Move) every "pulse" (tick).
    *   Only one Rite can be active at a time.
    *   **Interruption:** Movement or taking other strenuous actions breaks the channel.

## New Mechanics & Skills

### 1. Geomancy (Passive)
*   **Concept:** The Elemancer draws power from the land.
*   **Effect:** Invocations cost **20% less Will (Move)** when standing in a sector matching the element:
    *   **Fire:** Desert, Volcano.
    *   **Water:** Water, Underwater, Ocean.
    *   **Earth:** Hills, Mountains, Forest.
    *   **Air:** City, Field, Sky.

### 2. Elemental Attunement (Toggle/Stance)
*   **Command:** `attune <element>`
*   **Effect:** Shifts the Elemancer's internal balance.
    *   **Fire:** +Damroll, -AC.
    *   **Ice:** +AC, -Hitroll.
    *   **Earth:** +MaxHP (temp), -Dex.
    *   **Wind:** +MaxMove (temp), -Str.

### 3. Terraform (Active Skill)
*   **Command:** `terraform`
*   **Effect:** Temporarily alters the **Sector Type** of the current room to suit the Elemancer's needs.
    *   *Example:* Turns a `SECT_CITY` room into `SECT_DESERT` (Heat) or `SECT_FIELD` (Nature).
    *   **Utility:** Enables "Geomancy" bonuses anywhere.
    *   **Combat:** Can disrupt enemy pathing or give elemental advantages.
